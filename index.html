Quick Biology Revision

Cell theory: All living organisms are composed of cells; the cell is the basic structural and functional unit of life. New cells arise from pre-existing cells.

Enzymes: Most enzymes are proteins that act as biological catalysts. They lower activation energy and are not consumed in the reaction.

Photosynthesis: In green plants, light reactions occur in thylakoid membranes; the Calvin cycle occurs in the stroma of chloroplasts.
